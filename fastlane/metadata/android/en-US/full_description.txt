Squeeze compresses your videos to share them more easily or to get your storage back. Everything happens on your phone: no account, no internet permission, no tracking.

<b>Compress</b>
• Share one or many videos to Squeeze from the gallery, or pick them in the app
• Built-in presets (share quickly, balanced archive, maximum savings) and your own, with every FFmpeg parameter, a live command preview and a size estimate
• Runs in the background with a progress notification; one file at a time

<b>When it's done</b>
• Reshare the results, save them next to the originals, or replace the originals (with a check that the new file is good before anything is overwritten)
• Dates, albums and favourites are kept; location is optional

<b>History and stats</b>
• Every batch with sizes, savings, preset and the exact command used
• Totals per week, month and year, per preset

<b>Auto compression</b>
• Compress old videos overnight or while charging, with a different preset by video age
• Dry run, battery and charger conditions, skipped folders
• Optional automation API (token protected) for Tasker, MacroDroid and Samsung Routines

<b>Free software</b>
Squeeze is licensed under the GNU GPL v3 and includes FFmpeg with x264 and x265.
